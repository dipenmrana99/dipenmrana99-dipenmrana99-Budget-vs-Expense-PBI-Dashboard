The best ways to get Icons for your dashboards.

Material Design Icons:
https://material.io/resources/icons
Find the icon you want, click on the icon you want and download it as a png!

Font Awesome:
https://fontawesome.com/icons?d=gallery
Find the icons you want and then use the screenshot feature in your browser to save the icon as an image.

Flaticon
https://www.flaticon.com/packs/material-design
Download in multiple colors!
